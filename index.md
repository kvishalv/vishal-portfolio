## About

I'm an engineering leader and a Google Cloud certified Generative AI Leader. Most of my career has gone into building production systems the hard way: backend services, distributed architectures, payments, and the software that keeps them correct at scale.

Most teams reaching for AI are missing the discipline that makes it safe in production: idempotency, retries, reconciliation, month-end correctness. I bring that to the new layer, with production RAG, agentic systems, and AI-assisted development across engineering teams. I design systems, set technical standards, and build alongside the teams I run.
